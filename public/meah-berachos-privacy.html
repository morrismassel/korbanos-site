Privacy Policy — Meah Berachos

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Meah Berachos (“the App”) is built by Morris Massel (korbancalculator.com). This policy explains what the App does — and does not — collect.

The short version: The App has no accounts and no login. Your berachos counts and settings are stored on your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

What’s stored on your device

  • Your daily counts, streak, nusach, and preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • If you enable iCloud, this information is also backed up to your own private iCloud account (via Apple’s iCloud key-value storage) so it syncs across your devices. That data is controlled by your Apple ID; we have no access to it.

Location

  • If you allow location access, your approximate location is used to calculate the halachic day boundary (tzeis) and zmanim.
  • To show zmanim and candle-lighting / Havdalah times, your coordinates are sent to the Hebcal API (hebcal.com), which returns times for your location. Location is used only for this purpose, is not stored by us, and is not linked to your identity.
  • You can use the App without granting location; some time-based features will be limited.

Community count

The App shows an optional shared “community tally.” When you add berachos, the App may send an anonymous increment — just a number — to our counter service. It contains no name, email, location, nusach, or personal history; only the count being added.

Third-party services

The App connects to these services to provide content. It does not share personal information with them beyond what is noted:

  • Hebcal (hebcal.com) — zmanim and Daf Yomi; receives approximate location only for zmanim.
  • Sefaria (sefaria.org) — liturgical text; no personal data.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the anonymous community-count service.
  • Apple — iCloud backup (your account) and App Store distribution.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because there are no accounts, there is no server-side personal data to delete. To remove local data, use the App’s “Reset All Data” option or delete the App. iCloud data is managed in your device’s iCloud settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@korbancalculator.com
